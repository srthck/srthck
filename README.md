## About

B.Tech Computer Science student at MIT ADT University, Pune (Batch 2028), building backend and AI systems end to end — REST APIs, database and schema design, and computer-vision pipelines from data to deployment. Recent focus has shifted from isolated CRUD projects toward systems that carry real operational weight: a production dashboard used by an actual transport business, and payroll architecture designed to hold up under statutory audit.

Working toward backend / AI engineering roles at London-based fintechs (Revolut, Wise, Monzo), which is why the projects below increasingly emphasize correctness, auditability, and idempotency over feature count.

**What I actually do:**
- Design backend services and data models in FastAPI / Node.js with PostgreSQL or MongoDB, not just wire up CRUD.
- Build offline-first, realtime mobile and web clients (React Native, React 19, Capacitor, SSE).
- Ship computer-vision pipelines (YOLOv8, OpenCV) from a raw dataset to a working detection/classification model.
- Treat data integrity as a design constraint — immutable records, versioned business rules, append-only audit trails — not an afterthought.

## Featured Work

### AURA — AI-Driven Fitness & Nutrition Coach
Offline-first mobile app with a unified plan orchestrator that reconciles workout load, fatigue, and nutrition state instead of running three disconnected trackers. Currently hardening the orchestration and persistence layer ahead of new product surfaces (Nutrition, Home, Explore).
`React Native` `FastAPI` `SQLite` `Offline-first`

### Shivam Transport — Logistics Operations Dashboard
Full-stack operations dashboard in live production use for a transport business — trips, revenue, and collections tracking, realtime updates over Server-Sent Events, and an offline trip-submission queue built for fieldwork with unreliable connectivity. Packaged to Android/iOS with Capacitor.
`React 19` `TypeScript` `Node.js` `MUI` `Capacitor` `SSE`

### MSME Payroll & Statutory Compliance Platform
University systems-design project: payroll modeled as an accounting ledger rather than a monthly calculation — immutable payroll snapshots, versioned PF/ESI/PT compliance rules, per-deduction explainability, an append-only financial ledger, and idempotent payroll runs. Built for Indian MSMEs — small businesses, clinics, manufacturing units, coaching institutes.
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `React/TS` `Docker`
